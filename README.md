
#  Discover Jordan – Digital Tourism Platform



## About the Project

**Discover Jordan** is a digital tourism platform that aims to showcase **hidden gems and authentic local experiences**—moving beyond the well-known sites such as **Petra, Wadi Rum, and Aqaba**.

Our goal is to **promote domestic tourism** and **encourage authentic exploration** through:

* Interactive travel content
* User-generated stories
* Community-driven recommendations


## Mission

To redefine how people explore Jordan—**connecting travelers with authentic, lesser-known destinations** and supporting local communities through sustainable tourism.


## Features

* Explore interactive maps of hidden spots
* Share and read real traveler stories
* Discover local guides and experiences
* Engage with a community of explorers
